### About Me 

Curiosity is what led me to networking, and it's what keeps me learning every day. I enjoy understanding how networks are designed, how devices communicate, and how reliable infrastructure is built.

I'm currently learning Cisco CCNA v7 through Cisco Networking Academy and applying those concepts by developing enterprise networking projects.
